**Full description:**
How fast is your memory?

Numbers appear on the grid and the clock starts at once. Tap 1, and every other number turns into a blank white tile. Now tap the rest in order, from memory. One wrong tap and the round is over.

THREE MODES
• Easy: 5 numbers
• Medium: 9 numbers
• Hard: 15 numbers

GLOBAL LEADERBOARDS
Every mode has its own all-time leaderboard on Google Play Games. Finish a round and see your rank in the world.

SHORT AND SHARP
A round takes seconds. Beat your best, share your time, try once more.

Known as the "chimp test": the number-memory challenge that chimpanzees famously ace. Can you beat them?

No account needed. Ads between rounds can be removed with a one-time purchase.
